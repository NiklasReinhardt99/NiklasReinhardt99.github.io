I am a third-year PhD student at Heidelberg University in the group of Jakob Zech. I work at the intersection of scientific computing, statistics, and machine learning.

My current research focuses on developing and analyzing machine learning architectures that act as surrogate models for (infinite-dimensional) maps. In particular, I implement and analyze so-called operator learning architectures to solve parameter-dependent partial differential equations.

I studied mathematics and physics at Heidelberg University and KIT, specializing in numerical analysis and cosmology.

See my CV and my publications.
